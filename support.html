Need Help?

To use Gmail with WishMailer, you need to enable 2-Step Verification in your Google Account settings and then generate an App Password. Use this App Password instead of your regular email password in WishMailer.

Check your internet connection and ensure your email credentials are correct. If you are using Gmail, make sure you are using an App Password, not your login password. Also, check if your firewall is blocking the application.

The database is stored locally on your machine in the application's data folder. We do not store any of your data on our servers.
